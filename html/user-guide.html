How each control works

Selectable text — highlight any line or use Copy all steps.

  1. 1

    Click the TomatoInsight logo to return to the top of the landing page (Home). Opens: Home / top of landing page

  2. 2

    Click How it works to scroll to the three-step process: map farm, scan leaf, treat and track. Opens: How it works section (#how)

  3. 3

    Click Features to scroll to farm blueprint, guarded scans, classification, care tips, and history. Opens: Features section (#features)

  4. 4

    Click Diseases to scroll to Healthy, Yellow leaf curl, Early blight, and Spotted wilt cards. Opens: Diseases section (#diseases)

  5. 5

    Click Log in in the top bar if you already have an account. Opens: Sign-in page (login.html)

  6. 6

    Click Create account in the top bar to register a new account. Opens: Sign-up panel on the login page

  7. 7

    Click the hero Create account button to start registration from the main call to action. Opens: Sign-up panel on the login page

  8. 8

    Click the hero Log in button to sign in from the main call to action. Opens: Sign-in page (login.html)

  9. 9

    Use the leaf sample carousel arrows or dots to browse example tomato leaf photos. Shows: sample leaf images on the landing page
